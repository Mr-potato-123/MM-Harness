# Statistical Modeling

Define the population, sampling unit, outcome, predictors, estimand, and whether the goal is description, prediction, or causal inference. Match likelihood and link to support and measurement process. State independence, missing-data, functional-form, and identifiability assumptions.

Use a simple baseline before interactions, nonlinear terms, hierarchical structure, or regularization. Prevent leakage in preprocessing and model selection. Report effect or prediction scales with uncertainty, not p-values alone. Diagnose residuals, calibration, influence, collinearity, overdispersion, heteroscedasticity, and subgroup performance as applicable.

Validation must reproduce splits or resampling, compare baselines, and test material assumptions. Do not use observational association as causal effect without an identified design. Record convergence failures and sensitivity to specification.
