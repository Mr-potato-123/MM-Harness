# Geospatial Modeling

Record coordinate reference system, datum, geometry validity, spatial unit, temporal reference, resolution, coverage, and transformation lineage. Reproject before distance or area calculations and never mix degrees with metric distances.

Match method to support: points, networks, rasters, polygons, or areal aggregates. Check spatial autocorrelation, edge effects, uneven sampling, modifiable areal unit effects, ecological fallacy, and leakage between nearby train/test observations. Compare against a spatially naive baseline.

Validate topology, joins, distance calculations, held-out regions, and sensitivity to scale or neighborhood definition. Maps must show units, legend, projection/extent, uncertainty or missingness where material, and must not imply precision beyond the data.
