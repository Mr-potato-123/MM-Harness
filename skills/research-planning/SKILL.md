# Research Planning

Turn the problem into research facets before retrieving sources. Cover formulation, candidate methods, data requirements, validation, implementation, and known failure modes. Begin broad, then derive narrower follow-up queries from actual gaps; do not repeat synonymous searches without a reason.

For each query record its purpose, expected evidence, source class, and stopping condition. Prefer primary or local authoritative material. Compare independent sources for consequential claims and separate current facts from timeless method descriptions. A snippet is a lead, not full evidence.

Stop when the modeling choice and validation plan would not materially change with another routine query, or when the remaining gap is explicit. Return a compact source-linked synthesis, contradictions, coverage gaps, and recommended modeling implications—not a transcript of searches.
