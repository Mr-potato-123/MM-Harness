# Graph and Network Modeling

Define what nodes and edges mean, how duplicates/self-loops/missing entities are handled, and whether the graph is directed, weighted, signed, multipartite, spatial, or temporal. Preserve construction provenance; network conclusions can change radically with edge definition.

Choose metrics and algorithms that answer the task. Compare centrality against simple degree baselines, community results across resolution or seeds, and paths/flows against direction and capacity assumptions. Do not interpret descriptive topology as causal influence.

Validate on small hand-checkable graphs, disconnected components, isolates, weight scaling, and perturbations to uncertain edges. Report coverage, component structure, algorithm parameters, stability, and computational limits alongside figures.
