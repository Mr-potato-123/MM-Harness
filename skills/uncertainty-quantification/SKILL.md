# Uncertainty Quantification

Create an uncertainty inventory: measurement error, sampling variation, stochastic variability, parameter uncertainty, missing data, scenario uncertainty, and model-form ambiguity. Do not collapse distinct sources into one unexplained margin.

Choose propagation consistent with the model: analytic approximation, bootstrap, posterior simulation, ensembles, interval analysis, or Monte Carlo. Preserve correlations and constraints when sampling. Separate uncertainty conditional on the selected model from uncertainty across plausible models.

Report intervals or distributions with their interpretation, assumptions, coverage checks, and decision consequences. A narrow interval from an overconfident model is not strong evidence. Highlight uncertainty that was not quantified and test whether it changes the recommendation.
