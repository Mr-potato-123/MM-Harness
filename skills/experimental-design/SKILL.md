# Experimental Design

Define the experimental unit, treatment, control, outcome, estimand, assignment mechanism, blocking or stratification, sample-size rationale, stopping rule, and analysis plan before observing outcomes. Guard against pseudoreplication, interference, attrition, multiple testing, and measurement drift.

Use simulation or analytic power calculations with explicit effect sizes and variance assumptions. Preserve randomization seeds and allocation records. When randomization is impossible, state the identification assumptions and plausible confounders instead of calling the design experimental.

Report protocol deviations, missing outcomes, balance, uncertainty, and robustness to analysis choices. Exploratory analyses must be labeled separately from preregistered or confirmatory tests.
