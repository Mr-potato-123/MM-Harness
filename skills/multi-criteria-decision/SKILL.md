# Multi-Criteria Decision

Define alternatives, stakeholders, criteria, direction of preference, units, admissibility constraints, and missing-data rules. Remove duplicate or proxy criteria that double-count the same value. Explain every normalization and weight source.

Compare a transparent weighted baseline with an appropriate dominance, outranking, distance, or hierarchy method only when it changes the decision semantics. Test consistency of elicited judgments and distinguish compensatory from non-compensatory requirements.

Report score decomposition, dominated alternatives, weight and normalization sensitivity, rank reversals, ties, and uncertainty. A ranking is conditional on the declared preferences; do not present it as an objective fact.
