# Academic Paper Review

Identify the paper's question, claimed contributions, assumptions, method, evidence, baselines, uncertainty treatment, and stated limitations. Evaluate the paper that exists, not a different preferred project.

Check each major claim against the experiment, proof, dataset, or analysis that allegedly supports it. Examine leakage, confounding, baseline fairness, statistical power, ablations, robustness, reproducibility details, and whether conclusions exceed the studied regime. For theoretical work, inspect definitions, proof dependencies, boundary cases, and applicability.

Return a concise summary, specific strengths, major concerns, minor concerns, questions whose answers could change the verdict, and an evidence-calibrated recommendation. Separate verified defects from missing information. Keep tone professional and disclose any appendix, proof, data, or source that could not be inspected.
